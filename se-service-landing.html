Understanding Somatic Experiencing (SE)

Somatic Experiencing (SE) is a body-centered trauma healing modality focused on helping your nervous system metabolize shocking traumatic events or long-term, harmful stress trapped in the body.

Trauma is not "what happened" to you—it is how events were processed and held by your nervous system. Rather than focusing solely on talking through painful stories, SE works carefully across multiple channels (mind, body, nervous system, emotions, and meaning) to restore natural ease, resilience, and flow.

Somatic Concepts Made Simple: Core Metaphors

Nervous system science can sound complex, but the process of healing is deeply natural. Here is how we understand the work together:

🍉 1. "Eating a Watermelon in Pieces"

If you try to swallow a watermelon whole to get it over with, it’s unpleasant and overwhelming. But if you slice it up and eat it piece by piece, you can digest it with ease—and even enjoy the process! Trauma healing works the exact same way. We work gradually so your nervous system builds capacity without feeling overwhelmed.

⚖️ 2. "Regulation vs. Calm"

A well-regulated nervous system is not always still or calm. Being "regulated" means having the appropriate response to meet the present moment. Sometimes fight-or-flight is the healthiest, most adaptive response. SE helps your system respond appropriately when needed and return to balance afterward.

📦 3. "Expanding Your Container"

There is no such thing as "too much" stress—only a container that is currently too small. Our somatic work is designed not only to process past events, but to expand your personal container so future life stressors are less likely to stay trapped as trauma.

Who Benefits Most from Somatic Experiencing?

  • Shock Trauma & Chronic Stress: Individuals working through single-event traumatic stress, developmental trauma, or long-term burnout.
  • Idiopathic & Physical Symptoms: Clients living with chronic conditions such as Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome (EDS), POTS, Fibromyalgia, Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (CFS), or chronic migraines.
  • Activists, Leaders & Immigrants: Community organizers, movement leaders, refugees, and immigrants navigating systemic oppression, xenophobia, or collective stress.
  • Social Work & Liberation Framework: Grounded in anti-oppressive social work, Liberation Psychology, and systems theory.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is Somatic Experiencing (SE) therapy?

Somatic Experiencing (SE) is a body-centered trauma healing modality developed to help the nervous system process trapped survival stress and shocking events. Rather than focusing solely on talking through the story, SE works gently across mind, body, and emotions to restore natural regulation and resilience.

Do I have to re-tell my trauma story in session?

No. Many clients make significant progress in decreasing symptoms of trauma without re-telling the painful details. We focus on how your nervous system currently holds the experience rather than diving straight into painful memories.

How does SE help with chronic pain, EDS, POTS, or Fibromyalgia?

Long-term traumatic stress can manifest in physical body systems. For clients living with complex syndromes like EDS, POTS, CFS, or Fibromyalgia, gentle nervous system regulation can help decrease symptom intensity and improve overall quality of life alongside their medical care team.

Are services available in Spanish? (¿Servicios en español?)

Yes. Marakah Mancini de León provides all Somatic Experiencing and clinical social work sessions fluently in both English and Spanish at Odigo Wellness in Minneapolis.

Can I use FSA or HSA funds for sessions?

Yes. Many clients pay for private SE sessions with Flexible Spending Account (FSA) or Health Savings Account (HSA) funds. If you plan to use FSA/HSA, keep your receipt and check your plan's requirements for eligible provider types and documentation.

What age groups does Marakah work with?

Marakah works exclusively with adults aged 18 and older. For children or infants, she is happy to provide trusted referrals to specialized pediatric practitioners.
