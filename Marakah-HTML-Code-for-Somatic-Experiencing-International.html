About My Practice

Welcome. I’m Marakah Mancini de León (she/her), a Licensed Graduate Social Worker (LGSW), Somatic Experiencing Practitioner (SEP), and experienced clinical bodyworker based in Minneapolis.

My work centers on helping clients metabolize traumatic events, complex stress, and nervous system dysregulation so they can move forward with greater ease, resilience, and flow. Rather than viewing trauma or stress solely as a "story in the mind" or a "tight muscle in the body," I work across multiple channels—mind, body, emotions, and nervous system—to support holistic, lasting recovery.

Core Offerings & Modalities

Somatic Experiencing (SE) & Psychotherapy

A gentle, body-centered approach to trauma processing. SE works directly with the nervous system to process trapped survival energy without needing to retell painful stories. We gradually build your internal capacity, restore boundaries, and help your system move out of feeling "stuck on" (anxiety/hypervigilance) or "stuck off" (numbness/exhaustion).

(Available private-pay and through clinical social work/insurance workflows).

Clinical Bodywork & Hands-On Modalities

Craniosacral Therapy, Rehabilitative Massage, Lymphatic Support, and Reiki. These hands-on sessions focus on physical recovery, nervous system soothing, and body integration.

(Note: Emotional and trauma processing sessions are directed into Somatic Experiencing).

Who Benefits Most from This Work

  • Trauma & Stress Recovery: Individuals navigating shock trauma, complex PTSD, developmental stress, or burnout.
  • Idiopathic & Chronic Symptoms: Clients living with chronic fatigue (CFS), fibromyalgia, Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome (EDS), POTS, or migraines seeking gentle nervous system support alongside their care team.
  • Activists, Community Leaders & Immigrants: Dedicated community members, refugees, and leaders navigating systemic stress, oppression, or life transitions.

Our Approach & Philosophy

  • Bilingual Care: All sessions and consultations are offered fluently in English and Spanish.
  • Anti-Oppressive & Systems-Informed: Rooted in Liberation Psychology, systems theory, and community care.
  • Paced & Gentle: We work like "eating a watermelon in pieces"—slowly and intentionally, ensuring your system is resourced rather than overwhelmed.
