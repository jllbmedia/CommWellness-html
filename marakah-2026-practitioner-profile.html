About Marakah

Marakah offers Somatic Experiencing (SE)—including body-centered trauma processing—as well as Craniosacral Therapy, Rehabilitative Massage Therapy, Lymphatic Work, and Reiki.

Marakah loves working with people of all genders and cultural backgrounds who are ready to heal themselves. She is continually amazed and delighted by the creative solutions that clients' systems develop—and the way healing continues long after clients leave her treatment room.

Gentle, Paced Trauma Work: Trauma processing works best when approached carefully—like "eating a watermelon in pieces" rather than all at once. By building your system's capacity step by step, we help you process trapped survival stress without feeling overwhelmed.

Her clinical and bodywork practice has been informed by each client she has worked with, as well as by mentors and peers at Somatic Experiencing International, the Upledger Institute, Augsburg University, the Center for Victims of Torture, Park Nicollet, the Heartwood Institute, Northwestern Health Sciences University, Klose Training, Healing from the Core, Minka Brooklyn, Reiki by Esther, and the Third Root Community Health Center.

Marakah’s work is deeply rooted in anti-oppressive practice, Liberation Psychology, and systems theory. It is informed by the lived experiences of working people she has supported and organized alongside through decades of dedication to the labor movement, the cooperative movement, and social justice collective healing work.

Languages: Marakah is fluent in English and Spanish, comfortable in Portuguese, and communicates basic concepts in French and Somali.

She completed her 3-year Somatic Experiencing Practitioner (SEP) training in Spring 2025 and serves as an assistant for new SE student cohorts in English and Spanish (with Somatic Experiencing International and Uruguay's Fénix Instituto). She holds a Master's in Social Work (MSW) in Anti-Oppressive Clinical Practice from Augsburg University and practices as a Licensed Graduate Social Worker (LGSW).

Client Note: Marakah works with adults over the age of 18. If you are searching for a specialist for an infant or youth in your life, Marakah is happy to provide referrals to skilled practitioners specializing in younger age groups.
